# Persona Review

Review the final resume as four personas. Each persona catches what the others miss.

## 1. The Parser

The ATS parser cares about keywords and structure. Review as the parser:

- [ ] Are all required skills mentioned explicitly?
- [ ] Are job titles close to the role title in the JD?
- [ ] Are years of experience stated clearly?
- [ ] Are there any em-dashes or en-dashes?
- [ ] Is it single column with standard bullet points?
- [ ] Do degree and institution names match exactly?

## 2. The Recruiter

The recruiter has 6 seconds. They look for fit and red flags.

- [ ] Do the last one to two roles match the JD?
- [ ] Is the most relevant achievement in the top third?
- [ ] Are there unexplained employment gaps?
- [ ] Is there job-hopping or a pattern of short tenures?
- [ ] Does the summary make the case for why this person should get an interview?
- [ ] Are there obvious red flags (unrelated background, missing required skills)?

## 3. The Hiring Manager

The hiring manager will read the whole resume. They check if you can actually do the job.

- [ ] Is every claim verifiable?
- [ ] Are metrics real and did the candidate personally achieve them?
- [ ] Do the described roles match the responsibility level?
- [ ] Is there evidence of growth?
- [ ] Are the skills current (not 10 years old)?
- [ ] Does the resume tell a coherent story?

## 4. The Skeptical Interviewer

The interviewer will ask about every line. Can this candidate defend what they wrote?

For each major claim, imagine three follow-up questions:

**Claim:** "Led a team of 8 engineers to ship a real-time analytics platform."
- Q1: "What was your specific role? Did you hire, fire, and evaluate?"
- Q2: "What does 'real-time' mean in your platform?"
- Q3: "What is the most complex thing you personally built in it?"

If you cannot answer these questions as the candidate, the claim is too vague or overstated.

**Claim:** "Reduced database query latency by 45%."
- Q1: "What was the baseline? Was it milliseconds or seconds?"
- Q2: "What specific optimization did you do?"
- Q3: "How did you measure the improvement?"

If the answer is "the team did it" or "I was part of it," the claim should be reframed to reflect your actual role.

## The full review process

Before delivery:
1. Run the parser check (ATS-safe).
2. Imagine the recruiter's 6-second skim. Would they call you back?
3. Read as the hiring manager. Is this person qualified?
4. Read as the interviewer. Can they defend every claim?

If you fail any persona check, fix it before delivery. A resume that passes the ATS but fails the interview is a resume that wastes everyone's time.
