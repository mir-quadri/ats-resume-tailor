# Confirm Then Build

Ask the candidate to confirm gaps before rebuilding the resume. Never fill gaps by guessing.

## The questions

Ask one round of questions in this order:

1. **Every unverified metric.** "Your resume says X. Was that your work, your team's, or the department's?"
2. **Every date gap.** "There is a 6-month gap here. What was happening?"
3. **Retired claims.** "Anything here you no longer want to claim?"
4. **JD gaps.** "The JD asks for X. Do you have this, or is it a real gap?"
5. **Clarifications only.** Title, location, licensure — only if the source leaves them unclear.

Format: multiple-choice with a recommended default. See `assets/gap_questions_template.md`.

## The one-word confirmation rule

A one-word answer licenses exactly what was confirmed, nothing more.

**Example:**

Q: "Your resume says you led cost reduction from 40% to 22%. Was that your personal work?"
A: "Yes"

You may now use: "Led cost reduction from 40% to 22%"
You may NOT expand it to: "Led a strategic cost reduction initiative that resulted in moving company profitability metrics from 40% to 22%"

Q: "The JD asks for Kubernetes. Do you have production Kubernetes experience?"
A: "Some"

You may now use: "Kubernetes (limited production experience)"
You may NOT use: "Advanced Kubernetes expert with production deployments"

## Answering ambiguous responses

- "Mostly yes" → treat as yes.
- "Sort of" / "Some" → use as-is with qualifier.
- "No" → do not use.
- Vague or rambling → ask a follow-up, don't guess.

## Stop and wait

Your first turn ends after the flagged list, fit assessment, and questions. You do not write the resume yet. Wait for the candidate's answers before proceeding.

## Using the answers

After the candidate responds:
1. Update the ledger with confirmed facts.
2. Remove unverified metrics the candidate denies.
3. Build the banned list from retired claims.
4. Map the gaps they closed to the fit assessment.
5. Now build the resume using only confirmed material.

Never skip this cycle. A resume built on guesses is a resume that loses the interview.
