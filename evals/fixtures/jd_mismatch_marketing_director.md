# Senior Director, Brand Marketing Strategy
**Vantage Consumer Group | Chicago, IL (hybrid, 3 days on-site)**
**Base range: $185,000 - $240,000**

Vantage is looking for a Senior Director to own brand strategy across our North American
portfolio. You will lead a team of brand managers, own a $12M annual marketing budget, and
serve as the primary strategic partner to our commercial leadership.

## Responsibilities
- Own brand positioning, messaging architecture, and go-to-market strategy for three consumer brands.
- Lead and develop a team of 6 brand managers, including hiring, performance management, and compensation planning.
- Own and allocate a $12M annual marketing budget across paid, owned, and earned channels.
- Partner with agency teams on creative development and media planning.
- Present quarterly brand performance to the executive committee and the board.
- Commission and interpret consumer research, segmentation studies, and brand tracking.

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, or related field. MBA preferred.
- 12+ years in brand marketing, at least 5 leading brand teams at a consumer company.
- Demonstrated P&L or budget ownership at $10M+.
- Deep fluency in consumer research methodologies and brand health measurement.
- Agency management experience required.
