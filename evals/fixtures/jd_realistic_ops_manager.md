# Distribution Center Operations Manager
**Ridgeline Logistics | Sparks, NV (on-site)**
**Base range: $86,000 - $104,000**

Ridgeline is hiring an Operations Manager to run inbound and outbound operations at our
480,000 square foot Sparks distribution center, covering three shifts.

## Responsibilities
- Direct people management of 4 shift supervisors and indirect leadership of 90+ associates,
  including hiring, performance reviews, corrective action, and compensation recommendations.
- Own the site's safety program and maintain OSHA compliance and recordable incident targets.
- Administer and configure the site's warehouse management system, including user provisioning,
  wave strategy configuration, and troubleshooting integration errors with the corporate IT team.
- Own the site labor budget and report cost per unit to regional leadership monthly.
- Drive continuous improvement using structured problem solving and standard work.
- Manage inventory accuracy, cycle count programs, and annual physical inventory.
- Interface directly with key customer accounts on service escalations and performance reviews.

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree preferred, or equivalent experience.
- 5+ years in distribution or fulfillment operations, including 2+ years of direct people management.
- Experience administering a warehouse management system, not just operating one.
- Lean or Six Sigma certification preferred (Green Belt or above).
- Demonstrated budget ownership.
- OSHA certification required.
