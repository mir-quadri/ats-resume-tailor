JOB POSTING: Senior Data Engineer

Company: CloudScale Inc
Location: San Francisco, CA (Remote-first)

About the Role

We are looking for a Senior Data Engineer to join our growing data platform team. You will own the
design and implementation of real-time data pipelines that power our analytics and ML infrastructure.
This is a high-visibility role where your work directly impacts how the company makes data-driven decisions.

Requirements

REQUIRED:
- 5+ years of experience building production data pipelines
- Hands-on expertise with Apache Kafka or equivalent real-time streaming technology
- Strong SQL and Python skills
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Demonstrated ability to lead and mentor engineers

NICE TO HAVE:
- Production experience with Apache Spark
- Experience building data quality frameworks
- Background with at least one modern data warehouse (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift)
- Open-source contributions to data engineering projects

Responsibilities

- Design and implement scalable, real-time data pipelines serving multiple data consumers
- Own the quality and reliability of production data infrastructure
- Lead the technical architecture decisions for the data platform
- Mentor junior engineers and establish best practices for the team
- Partner with analytics and ML teams to understand data requirements
- Optimize data processing costs and performance

About Us

CloudScale is a Series B data infrastructure company. Our mission is to make data accessible and
reliable for businesses of all sizes. We value technical excellence, pragmatism, and continuous learning.
