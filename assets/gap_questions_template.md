# Gap Questions Template

Ask these questions after the fit assessment and flagged list, before writing the resume.

Format: multiple-choice with a recommended default. Wait for answers before proceeding.

---

## UNVERIFIED METRICS

For each metric flagged in the fact ledger:

**Q1. Your resume says "the team reduced shrink 30%". Was that your personal work, your team's collective result, or the department's result?**
- A: My personal work
- B: My team's result (I was part of the effort)
- C: Department or company result
- D: Mixed (recommend specific breakdown)

Recommended default: B

---

**Q2. You list "led code review practice for 4 engineers". Did you formally manage these engineers, or was this a peer practice you drove?**
- A: Formal management (hired, evaluated, coached)
- B: Peer practice I drove (no direct reports)
- C: Shared responsibility with someone else

Recommended default: B

---

**Q3. Your resume says "reduced query latency by 45%". What was the baseline and how did you measure it?**
- A: [Your answer]

(Open response for clarification on vague metrics.)

---

## DATE GAPS

For each employment gap over 2 months:

**Q4. There is a 6-month gap between Role A (ended June 2022) and Role B (started January 2023). What was happening during this time?**
- A: Job search
- B: Education or training
- C: Sabbatical or unpaid leave
- D: Contract work (brief details)
- E: [Your explanation]

Recommended default: A (job search is common, needs no explanation)

**Action if "other":** If the candidate explains, you can mention it briefly in the resume or simply leave the gap with no explanation. A gap needs no excuse if the timeline is reasonable.

---

## JD GAPS

For each gap identified in the fit assessment:

**Q6. The JD asks for Kubernetes. Do you have production Kubernetes experience, or is this a gap you're willing to ramp on?**
- A: Strong Kubernetes experience (production, 2+ years)
- B: Some Kubernetes experience (limited production)
- C: No Kubernetes, but willing to learn
- D: [Your explanation]

Recommended default: C

---

**Q7. The role expects "Python with strong SQL". Where are you strong and where are you weaker?**
- A: Both equally strong (5+ years each)
- B: Stronger in Python, moderate SQL
- C: Stronger in SQL, moderate Python
- D: [Your breakdown]

Recommended default: [Based on resume]

## CLARIFICATIONS (only if source resume is ambiguous)

**Q8. Your resume shows "2015–2020" for your degree. Is that graduation year or graduation year missing? What degree did you earn?**
- A: Graduated in 2015 (B.S. Computer Science)
- B: [Your clarification]

Recommended default: Assume graduation is the end date.

## SENIORITY AND TITLE

(Only if the source resume is vague or ambiguous):

**Q9. For this role, would you position yourself as an individual contributor, a team lead, or a manager? Or something else?**
- A: Individual contributor (hands-on, no direct reports)
- B: Tech lead (hands-on + peer mentorship, no direct reports)
- C: Manager (team responsibility, direct reports)
- D: Hybrid (mix of the above)

Recommended default: [Based on fit assessment and job description]

---

## Rules for answers

- **One-word answers expand to exactly that.** "Yes" = one confirmation. Do not elaborate.
- **Vague answers get a follow-up.** "Sort of" = you can use it with a qualifier, or ask for more detail.
- **No answers get omitted.** "No" = the skill/claim does not go in the resume.
- **Non-answers require a follow-up.** A rambling response means the candidate is unsure. Ask more specifically.

## After you receive answers

1. Update the fact ledger with confirmed ownership.
2. Remove denied or retired claims.
3. Build the banned list from retired claims.
4. Re-assess fit based on closed gaps.
5. Pick your positioning lane.
6. Write the resume using only confirmed material.

Never proceed to writing without these answers. The answers are the guardrail against hallucination.
