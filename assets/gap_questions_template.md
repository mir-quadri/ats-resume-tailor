# Question Patterns

Two rounds. Intake first, gaps after the fit assessment. See `references/confirm-then-build.md`.

## Round one: intake

Ask only what the source resume does not already answer.

> Is the title on this posting the one you want mirrored at the top, or should I use a different
> target title?

> This role is [on-site / hybrid / remote] in [location]. Does that work?
> - Yes
> - Yes, I would relocate
> - No, skip it

> I see a gap in your dates between [date] and [date]. How do you want it handled?
> - It was [reason], say so plainly
> - Leave it unexplained
> - The dates are wrong, here is the correction

> Is there anything on your current resume you no longer want to claim? Numbers you cannot
> substantiate, a title that overstates the role, a result that was really the team's.

That last question builds the banned-claims list. Ask it once, at intake, on every new candidate.

## Round two: gap questions

Batch these into a single round. Three questions maximum. Each answerable by choosing an option,
each with a recommended default, each including an honest opt-out.

## 1. The decision question (only after a conditional fit read)

> Given that fit read, do you want me to build it?
> - Build it, I will take the down-level
> - Build it, but only if the gaps can be framed honestly
> - Skip it

Ask this first. Everything else is moot if the answer is skip.

## 2. The missing-experience question

> The JD wants [requirement]. Do you have [specific thing]?
> - Yes, directly, and I owned it
> - Yes, but adjacent or at smaller scope
> - No
> - Not sure, leave it out

Never expand a "yes" beyond the words in the option they chose. See `confirm-then-build.md`.

## 3. The currency question

> How should I frame [skill they once had]?
> - Current and hands-on today
> - Used it historically, not current
> - Leave it off entirely

Overstated currency is the fastest way to fail a technical screen.

## 4. The ownership question

Use whenever a source-resume metric looks like it might belong to a broader effort.

> Your resume shows [metric]. Was that your work, your team's work, or the program's result?
> - Mine, I drove it
> - My team, I led them
> - The broader program, I contributed
> - Not sure, drop it

"The broader program" means rescope the bullet. "Not sure" means it goes in `banned.txt`.

## 5. The seniority and constraints question

> This role is posted at [level] with a band of [range], based in [location].
> - Fine, build it
> - Build it but position me at their level
> - That is a dealbreaker, skip it
