### Role 1: Senior Data Engineer
- Company: TechCorp

- Pipeline rebuild: Migrated from batch to real-time (6 months, 2021)

### Role 2: Data Engineer
- Company: DataStartup
- Dates: June 2018 to December 2020 (2.5 years)
- ETL pipeline: Built 12 data pipelines for sales analytics
- Data quality: Reduced data quality incidents by 60%
- Tools: Airflow, Python, PostgreSQL, Tableau (2018-2020)

## Skills

- Python: 6 years (2018-present)
- Kafka: 3 years (2020-present)
- Apache Spark: 2 years (2021-present)
- SQL: 6 years

## Notes

- **UNVERIFIED:** "Query performance: Reduced Redshift query time" — was this personal work or the team's?
- **Scope language:** "Mentored 2 junior engineers" (mentorship, not direct management)
- **Currency check:** Tableau last used 2020, 5 years ago. Confirm if still relevant?
- **Date gap:** December 2020 to January 2021 (1 month) — brief, no concern

---

Rules:
1. Transcribe facts exactly as they appear in the source.
2. Use neutral language. "Led", "built", "achieved" are fine. "Grew", "optimized", "transformed" (without specifics) are vague.
3. Record the exact number: "50M events per day", not "high volume".
4. Mark ownership ambiguities as UNVERIFIED.
5. Note the last year for skills, not "expert" or "intermediate".
6. Never add facts from memory. Source resume only.
